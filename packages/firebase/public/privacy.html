Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2024

This Privacy Policy explains how Dreamio - AI Art Generator ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our App.

This App is developed and maintained by Ilsur Gabdulkhakov. If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at support@ilsur.me.

1. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personal data beyond what is necessary for the functionality of the App. We collect:

  • Anonymous User ID: Managed via Firebase Authentication.
  • Prompt Data: We store prompts used to generate images and the resulting images, linked to the anonymous Firebase user ID.
  • Purchase Data: We store information related to in-app purchases and subscriptions, linked to the Firebase anonymous user ID.

2. Data Usage

We use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Adapty SDK to track anonymous usage data, manage in-app purchases, and improve app performance. No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected or shared beyond what is necessary for these services.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Adapty SDK: We share the anonymous Firebase Auth user ID with Adapty to manage purchases. No other personal data is transmitted.
  • OpenAI: Only the prompt submitted for image generation is transmitted to OpenAI. No personal user data is shared with OpenAI.

4. Data Storage and Global Use

Data collected by the App may be processed and stored in different regions, depending on the services we use, such as Firebase (Google), OpenAI, and Adapty. All data is stored securely and in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

5. User Rights

Users can request the deletion of their data or information at any time by contacting us at support@ilsur.me. We will ensure that all data, including generated prompts and purchases, is deleted in a timely manner.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with a new effective date.
