Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 12, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Dreamio - AI Art Generator ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our App.

This App is developed and maintained by Ilsur Gabdulkhakov. If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us at support@ilsur.me.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not intentionally collect or store personal data. All data, including prompts and generated content, is stored locally on the user’s device. The App communicates directly with Pollinations.AI for image generation, and no user data is processed or retained by Dreamio.

2. Data Usage

The App does not utilize third-party analytics, crash reporting, or purchase management tools. All interactions are limited to direct communication with Pollinations.AI’s API for generating images.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Pollinations.AI: The App transmits only the prompt submitted for image generation to Pollinations.AI. No personal user data is shared with Pollinations.AI.

4. Data Storage and Global Use

Since no server-side data storage is implemented, all user data remains on the user’s device. Data transmitted to Pollinations.AI is subject to their privacy policies.

5. User Rights

Users control all data stored locally on their devices. To delete generated content or data, users may manually clear the storage on their device. For any assistance, users can contact us at support@ilsur.me.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with a new effective date.
