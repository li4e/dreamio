Subscription Terms

Effective Date: September 11, 2024

These Subscription Terms outline the rules and conditions for managing subscriptions within Dreamio - AI Art Generator ("App").

1. Subscription Auto-Renewal

Subscriptions in the App are automatically renewed at the end of the subscription period. This includes any free trial period if applicable. Users may cancel the subscription at any time, but cancellation must occur before the renewal date to avoid charges.

2. Free Trial Period

Some users may have access to a free trial period for the subscription. If the subscription is not canceled before the trial period ends, the user will be charged for the full price of the subscription at the end of the trial period.

3. How to Cancel Your Subscription

To cancel a subscription, you must use the cancellation functions provided by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Follow the instructions below based on your device:

  • Apple (iOS) Users:
    1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
    2. Tap your Apple ID at the top of the screen, then tap Subscriptions.
    3. Find and tap on the subscription for Dreamio - AI Art Generator.
    4. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm your choice.
  • Google Play (Android) Users:
    1. Open the Google Play Store app.
    2. Tap on your profile icon in the top right corner.
    3. Tap Payments & subscriptions and then Subscriptions.
    4. Find the Dreamio - AI Art Generator subscription and tap Cancel Subscription.

4. Refunds and Payment Issues

For refunds or any payment disputes, you must contact Apple or Google Play support directly. Ilsur Gabdulkhakov is not responsible for managing refunds or handling payment disputes, as all payments are processed by the app stores.

5. Expiration of Generations

All generations purchased with the subscription will expire at the end of the subscription period. Unused generations do not roll over to the next period and will be lost upon expiration.
